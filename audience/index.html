06 / STRATEGY & ASSETS

Audience & Markets

Understanding who buys, who influences commercial decisions, and how LevelUp connects brands with sports fans across MENA and South Asia.

Ecosystem Stakeholder Groups

LevelUp Media serves four core groups within the global sports marketing ecosystem:

GROUP 01

Rights Holders & Leagues

Seeking reliable commercial monetization, high broadcast quality, and digital distribution expansion.

GROUP 02

Global & Regional Brands

Seeking direct access to high-intent cricket and football fans without five agency middlemen.

GROUP 03

Creators & Athletes

Seeking professional representation, brand sponsorship alignment, and high-end video production support.

GROUP 04

Digital & OTT Platforms

Seeking high-retention live sports highlights, stats widgets, and interactive fan engagement features.

Decision Makers — Buyers vs. Influencers

Tailor commercial communications specifically to the priorities of each decision-maker role:

THE BUYERS (WHO PAYS)

CMOs, VPs of Marketing, Heads of Commercial

Primary Goal: Measurable business growth, direct audience reach, and contract simplicity.

Communication Focus: Lead with numbers (400M+ reach, 150+ brands), custom inventory, and single-contract execution.

THE INFLUENCERS (WHO RECOMMENDS)

Media Planners, Sponsorship Directors, Brand Leads

Primary Goal: Seamless implementation, transparent reporting, and turnkey asset delivery.

Communication Focus: Provide clear collateral specs, booked vs. delivered reporting, and fast asset turnarounds.

COMMERCIAL HEADQUARTERS

Dubai, United Arab Emirates

MENA & South Asia

Key Pillars

International Cricket, Football Leagues, Combat & Esports.
