Clearspace & Minimum Sizes

To maintain visual integrity, always keep clear space around the logo free from typography, competing graphics, and edge margins.

1U CLEARSPACE MARGIN

Clearspace distance (1U) equals the exact height of the standalone U icon mark.

Minimum Scale Limits

Format Standalone U Symbol LEVELUP Wordmark
Digital (Screen) 24px height 120px width
Print Collateral 8mm height 35mm width

Approved Background Pairings

Only place the logo on approved brand color fields to maintain contrast and identity recognition:

Deep Navy (#070732)
Blue Orchid (#3343FF)
White (#FFFFFF)
Electric Green (#A6E35F)

Logo Misuse — What NOT To Do

To preserve brand equity, never alter the logo in any of the following ways:

✕ DO NOT STRETCH
Do not distort or stretch width and height proportions.
✕ DO NOT RECOLOR
Do not use unauthorized brand colors or custom fills.
✕ DO NOT ROTATE
Do not slant, tilt, or rotate the logotype angle.
✕ DO NOT ADD EFFECTS
Do not add drop shadows, bevels, or outer glows.
✕ NO LOW CONTRAST
Do not place logo on low-contrast or busy patterns.
✕ DO NOT RE-TYPE
LEVELUP
Do not reconstruct logotype with standard fonts.
